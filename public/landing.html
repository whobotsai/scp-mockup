Two campaign types

Pay for the behavior that grows a token

Both mechanisms share the same promise: reach the target and the pool pays out to whoever earned it. Miss it, and the pool stays locked forever.

SHO

Strong Hold Offering

01
Lock a pool, set milestones
Creator picks market-cap targets and how much of the pool each one unlocks.
02
Traders buy, get ranked
Net-buy volume in a rolling window ranks the most active wallets on the leaderboard.
03
Milestone hits, pool pays out
A TWAP confirms the crossing, a 24h challenge window runs, then top traders claim their share.
SSO

Strong Shill Offering

01
Lock a pool, set a keyword
Creator defines a tracked keyword and how often the pool pays out, per epoch.
02
Shillers post, get scored
Registered accounts post using the keyword; engagement scores them each epoch.
03
Epoch closes, pool pays out
Top accounts by score split that epoch's share once the challenge window clears.
